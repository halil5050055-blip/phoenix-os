# Project Direction

The canonical Phoenix OS vision, scope boundaries, success metrics, and stakeholder decision rights are defined in [`docs/project-vision.md`](../docs/project-vision.md).

Durable constraints:

- Vertical 1 commercial operations remain the near-term product focus.
- New capabilities must improve a defined metric or remove a verified measurement blocker.
- Individual stakeholder assignments are not yet documented and must be recorded before a production pilot.
- Broader CRM workspaces, autonomous AI agents, microservices, and unrelated business-domain automation remain deferred until separately prioritized.

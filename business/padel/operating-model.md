## Purpose and Current Evidence

This model governs padel-related opportunities, partnerships, research, and operations. The repository does not yet identify a specific offering, geography, customer segment, budget, partner, or accountable individual; those are unknowns to validate during assessment.

## Domain Lifecycle

1. **Opportunity intake:** record the source, proposed beneficiary, location or market if known, and evidence state.
2. **Problem validation:** verify the user or operator need before selecting a solution.
3. **Feasibility assessment:** evaluate operating requirements, venue or partner dependencies, economics, legal considerations, and delivery capability that actually apply.
4. **Pilot authorization:** define the smallest reversible test, owner, budget authority, success measure, safety controls, and stop conditions.
5. **Pilot execution:** collect agreed evidence without expanding scope or making unapproved commitments.
6. **Outcome review:** decide to stop, revise, repeat, or scale based on the pilot target and unresolved risks.
7. **Operationalization or closure:** assign ongoing ownership and controls, or close obligations and preserve findings.

## Required Review Questions

- Is the opportunity supported by a verified need rather than enthusiasm alone?
- Who benefits, pays, operates, approves, and bears risk?
- Which venue, equipment, staffing, partner, insurance, regulatory, or safety assumptions require qualified review?
- What is the smallest pilot that can test the highest-risk assumption?
- Which costs and revenues are verified, quoted, inferred, or still unknown?
- What outcome would cause the initiative to stop rather than continue?

## Evidence and Metrics

Each initiative selects measures appropriate to its hypothesis. At minimum, track validation conversations or observations, pilot participation or usage, operator effort, direct approved cost, incidents or complaints, and the primary outcome named in the brief. Do not assign numeric targets without an accountable owner and evidence-based baseline or validation rationale.

## Required Domain Artifacts

- initiative brief and evidence log;
- partner or venue assumptions with sources;
- pilot plan and explicit authorization;
- risk and obligation list;
- outcome review and decision link;
- operating handoff or closure record.

Legal, safety, insurance, employment, tax, and regulatory conclusions require qualified review where applicable; internal research must not be presented as professional advice.

## Purpose and Current Evidence

This model governs China-related market research, partner exploration, localization, and operations. The repository does not currently verify a target region, customer segment, entity structure, product, partner, budget, or launch date. Work begins with research and accountable validation, not assumed market entry.

## Domain Lifecycle

1. **Question intake:** state the business question, proposed value, geographic scope if known, source, and decision it should inform.
2. **Evidence assessment:** gather attributable market, customer, partner, technical, and regulatory information; label translations and unverified reports.
3. **Feasibility and risk review:** identify localization, data, contracting, intellectual-property, payment, tax, licensing, security, and operating questions that apply.
4. **Partner or pilot due diligence:** define evaluation criteria, authority, information-sharing boundary, validation method, and stop conditions before external engagement.
5. **Pilot authorization:** approve a bounded, reversible activity with an owner, resources, required qualified reviews, and measurable outcome.
6. **Execution and monitoring:** preserve source provenance, commitments, issues, and changes in assumptions.
7. **Review and disposition:** stop, revise, expand research, proceed to another bounded pilot, or operationalize with explicit ownership and controls.

## Required Review Questions

- Is the information current, attributable, and specific to the relevant jurisdiction and activity?
- Was translated or second-hand material independently checked where the decision risk warrants it?
- Who is authorized to contact partners, share information, negotiate, or make commitments?
- Which legal, regulatory, tax, cybersecurity, data-transfer, licensing, or intellectual-property questions need qualified local advice?
- What partner claims remain reported rather than verified?
- What evidence would invalidate the opportunity or stop the pilot?

## Evidence and Metrics

Measure research coverage against the decision questions, source quality and date, unresolved critical unknowns, completion of required reviews, partner claims verified, approved pilot outcomes, and outstanding obligations. Commercial targets remain unset until an accountable owner establishes a sourced baseline or validation rationale.

## Required Domain Artifacts

- research question and source register;
- assumptions and unknowns log;
- translation provenance where applicable;
- partner evaluation and communication record using minimal necessary personal data;
- qualified-review questions and outcomes;
- pilot authorization and outcome review;
- commitments, obligations, handoff, or closure record.

Repository material is internal research, not legal, regulatory, tax, or other professional advice. High-impact conclusions and external commitments require the appropriately qualified and authorized reviewer.
